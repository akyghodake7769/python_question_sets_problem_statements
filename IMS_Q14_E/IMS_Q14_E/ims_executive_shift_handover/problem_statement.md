# Incident Management Lab: Executive Communication & Structured Shift Handover

Duration : 45 Min.

## Scenario
During a shift transition, 3 major incidents remain active. You must prepare a structured shift handover document for the oncoming team.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Prepare an Executive Business Update and conduct a structured shift handover covering active P1/P2 incidents and ETRs.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Summarizing active P1/P2 incident statuses, current workarounds, and ETRs: 3 marks
2. Structuring operational shift handover notes to ensure uninterrupted incident management: 3 marks
3. Preparing non-technical business impact updates for C-level executives: 2 marks
4. Verifying communication approvals and distribution lists for external broadcasts: 2 marks

**Total Score: 10 Marks**
