# Incident Management Lab: Third-Party Vendor Escalation & SLA Governance Audit

Duration : 90 Min.

## Scenario
A cloud database provider experienced an outage, but delayed incident notification by 3 hours, breaching vendor contract SLAs.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Audit vendor incident handoffs, construct vendor escalation paths, evaluate contract SLA compliance, and draft a vendor penalty report.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Auditing vendor ticket timelines to identify communication delays and SLA breaches: 3 marks
2. Constructing multi-tier vendor escalation matrices (Account Manager to VP of Engineering): 3 marks
3. Evaluating third-party SLA contracts against actual incident resolution metrics: 3 marks
4. Drafting formal vendor SLA breach notices and credit penalty claims: 3 marks
5. Establishing joint vendor-client incident bridge protocols for critical outages: 3 marks
6. Defining vendor architectural redundancy standards to prevent single point of failure: 2 marks
7. Structuring a vendor performance scorecard for executive QBR reviews: 3 marks

**Total Score: 20 Marks**
