# Incident Management Lab: Enterprise Incident Lifecycle & Incident-to-Problem Transformation

Duration : 90 Min.

## Scenario
An IT department is transitioning from ad-hoc incident handling to a mature ITIL v4 Incident & Problem Management process.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Design an end-to-end incident lifecycle model, configure ServiceNow workflow states, define PIR governance, and map KEDB workflows.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Designing an end-to-end ITIL v4 incident management process flow diagram: 3 marks
2. Configuring ServiceNow ticket state models, mandatory field gates, and auto-close timers: 3 marks
3. Establishing Priority Matrix rules aligning business impact with resolution SLAs: 3 marks
4. Structuring automated trigger criteria for converting major incidents to Problem Records: 3 marks
5. Designing KEDB publication workflows and knowledge article review lifecycles: 2 marks
6. Formulating Incident Management KPIs (MTTR, MTBF, First Contact Resolution, SLA Compliance): 2 marks
7. Developing training runbooks for Service Desk analysts and Incident Commanders: 2 marks
8. Authoring a complete Enterprise Incident & Problem Management Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): 2 marks

**Total Score: 20 Marks**
