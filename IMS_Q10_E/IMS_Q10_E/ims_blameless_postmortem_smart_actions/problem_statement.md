# Incident Management Lab: Blameless Postmortems & SMART Action Items

Duration : 30 Min.

## Scenario
An incident summary contains finger-pointing notes blaming junior developers. You must refactor it into a blameless postmortem.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Rewrite blame-oriented incident notes into a blameless postmortem report and convert recommendations into SMART action items.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Refactoring blame-oriented incident summaries into blameless postmortem reports: 3 marks
2. Identifying systemic process and automation flaws rather than human errors: 3 marks
3. Formulating Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound (SMART) action items: 2 marks
4. Establishing continuous improvement feedback loops for engineering teams: 2 marks

**Total Score: 10 Marks**
