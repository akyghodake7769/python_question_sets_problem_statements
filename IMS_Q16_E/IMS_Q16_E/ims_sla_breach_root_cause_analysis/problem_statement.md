# Incident Management Lab: SLA Breach RCA & Operational Resolution Review

Duration : 45 Min.

## Scenario
A P2 incident breached its 2-hour resolution SLA by 90 minutes. You must analyze the root causes of the delay.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Perform an SLA breach audit, analyze queue wait times, identify bottleneck handoffs, and recommend process improvements.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Analyzing incident queue assignment history to locate time-in-queue delays: 2 marks
2. Identifying bottlenecks in team-to-team ticket handoffs and vendor response times: 2 marks
3. Evaluating technical resolution steps to determine root causes of resolution delays: 2 marks
4. Formulating SLA recovery plans and automated escalation trigger adjustments: 2 marks
5. Documenting an SLA breach summary report for IT governance reviews: 2 marks

**Total Score: 10 Marks**
