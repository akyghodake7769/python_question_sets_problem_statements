# Incident Management Lab: Functional and Time-Based SLA Escalation Auditing

Duration : 60 Min.

## Scenario
A P2 database performance issue stalled for 4 hours without escalation due to broken assignment rules. You must audit escalation workflows.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Audit escalation timelines, configure functional/time-based escalation triggers, and establish vendor SLA management rules.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Auditing incident timelines to identify SLA breach points and escalation stalls: 5 marks
2. Configuring automated time-based escalation rules matching P1-P4 SLA thresholds: 5 marks
3. Structuring functional escalation paths across L1, L2, L3, and Architecture teams: 5 marks
4. Establishing third-party vendor escalation protocols and SLA tracking metrics: 5 marks

**Total Score: 20 Marks**
