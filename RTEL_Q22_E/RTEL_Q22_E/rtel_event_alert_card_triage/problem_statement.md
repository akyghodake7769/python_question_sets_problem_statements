# Observability Lab: Alert Card Severity Triage & Impact Assessment

Duration : 45 Min.

## Scenario
A monitoring system fires multiple concurrent problem notifications. You must evaluate alert severity and determine immediate response actions.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Analyze sample alert cards, assess business impact on dependent services, and document immediate operational remediation steps.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Evaluating alert card metadata to determine problem severity and business impact: 3 marks
2. Documenting immediate operational response actions for affected services: 3 marks
3. Mapping event alert triggers to component failure modes: 2 marks
4. Structuring incident notification broadcasts for on-call engineers: 2 marks

**Total Score: 10 Marks**
