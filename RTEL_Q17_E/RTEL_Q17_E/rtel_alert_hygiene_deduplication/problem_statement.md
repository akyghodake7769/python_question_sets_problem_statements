# Observability Lab: Alert Fatigue Reduction & Noise Deduplication Strategy

Duration : 30 Min.

## Scenario
An operations team receives over 500 alert notifications daily, leading to alert fatigue and missed critical P1 incidents.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Audit alert notification rules, implement anomaly detection, configure alert grouping/deduplication, and link operational runbooks.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Auditing existing alert rules to identify false positives and redundant triggers: 3 marks
2. Tuning static threshold alerts to use dynamic anomaly detection models: 3 marks
3. Configuring alert grouping, deduplication, and dependency suppression rules: 2 marks
4. Linking automated operational runbook URLs directly to alert notifications: 2 marks

**Total Score: 10 Marks**
