# Incident Management Lab: SRE Reliability Governance & Blameless Postmortem Overhaul

Duration : 90 Min.

## Scenario
An enterprise organization suffers from a culture of blame during incidents. You must transform postmortem practices using SRE principles.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Audit postmortem histories, refactor incident reports into blameless postmortems, establish Error Budget policies, and build SRE dashboards.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Auditing historical incident reports to identify blame-oriented language and culture gaps: 3 marks
2. Refactoring incident reviews into blameless postmortems focusing on systemic resilience: 3 marks
3. Defining Service Level Indicators (SLIs) and Service Level Objectives (SLOs) for critical services: 3 marks
4. Establishing Error Budget burn-rate policies and deployment freeze triggers: 3 marks
5. Formulating SMART corrective action items to improve platform fault tolerance: 2 marks
6. Designing SRE reliability tracking dashboards in Grafana/ServiceNow: 2 marks
7. Establishing continuous feedback loops between SRE, DevOps, and Product Management: 2 marks
8. Authoring an Enterprise Reliability & Incident Governance Framework document: 2 marks

**Total Score: 20 Marks**
