# Incident Management Lab: Production P1 Major Incident Bridge Command Simulation

Duration : 60 Min.

## Scenario
A production database corruption causes global app downtime. You must lead the Major Incident Bridge command through recovery.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Manage a simulated P1 bridge, coordinate technical SMEs, authorize emergency hotfix changes, and issue executive broadcasts.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Leading a P1 Major Incident Bridge, managing technical SMEs, and enforcing bridge etiquette: 5 marks
2. Authorizing Emergency Change Requests (ECR) for hotfix deployments under pressure: 5 marks
3. Drafting synchronized stakeholder broadcasts for Executive Leadership and Operations: 5 marks
4. Documenting complete bridge decision logs, timelines, and resolution sign-offs: 5 marks

**Total Score: 20 Marks**
